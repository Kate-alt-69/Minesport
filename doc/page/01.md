# Page 01 — Start Here

**Audience:** you have Minecraft, you want part of a world in Blender, and you do not want to learn every Minesport system before exporting once.

## The short version

Minesport reads a Minecraft world **without editing the save**, lets you choose an area, resolves Minecraft/block/mod geometry, and exports that area as glTF or OBJ. The Blender addon then translates Minesport metadata into Blender-friendly objects, FLATTER logical geometry, materials, animations, and lights.

The normal workflow is:

1. Open **Minesport**.
2. Click the **folder icon** on the left and choose **Open Minecraft world…**.
3. Select the area you want in the 2D world view or enter coordinates.
4. Click the **Export** activity on the left.
5. Choose **glTF 2.0** for the best Blender path unless you specifically need OBJ.
6. Choose an output folder.
7. Click **Export world**.
8. In Blender, use the Minesport importer/addon installed by Minesport.

You do **not** need a Minesport “Project” file. That system was removed for 0.2.0.
